The Khalis Manifesto

Why We Refuse to Boil, Blend, or Feed Sugar to Bees.

Most commercial honey on Pakistani shelves is heated to high temperatures to prevent crystallization, destroying live enzymes and vitamins. We started Khalis Bazaar to deliver honey as nature intended: raw, unpasteurized, and full of life.

Born from Frustration

STRAIGHT FROM FARMERS, ZERO COMPROMISE.

In 2021, when searching for authentic Sidr honey for our family, we discovered that over 80% of supermarket jars in Pakistan were blended with industrial high-fructose corn syrup, diluted with sugar water, or aggressively boiled to remain perpetually clear.

We traveled to the arid Beri valleys of Karak and the high-altitude orchards of Swat to forge direct relationships with generational beekeepers. We made a simple pact: we pay them well above market rates in exchange for a strict pledge—never feed sugar syrup during the floral bloom, never apply artificial heat, and let the bees forage exclusively in wild nature.

Direct at Hive Temperature

Wild Sidr hives in Karak are extracted between 28°C and 34°C, preserving active bee enzymes and natural pollen.

Quality Architecture

Our Four Purity Tenets

The standards behind every single jar shipped from our hubs.

1

Cold-Extracted

Never heated above 35°C. Heat destroys honey’s natural invertase, glucose oxidase, and delicate antioxidants.

2

Zero Sugar Feeding

Commercial beekeepers often feed sugar water to accelerate yield. Our harvest relies solely on wild blossoms.

3

Gravity Settled

We allow honey to settle naturally through stainless steel mesh, retaining microscopic bee pollen and propolis.

4

PCSIR Tested

Every seasonal batch is sent to government PCSIR labs to certify sucrose is 0.0% and moisture is below 17%.

Our Commitment

"If it is not pure enough for our children, it will not enter our jars."

We stand behind every gram of honey, walnut, and ghee. If any accredited lab ever proves our harvest adulterated, we will refund your payment in full and pay your testing fees.
